About Us

IDN E-Report is an official digital reporting and feedback platform designed to foster a transparent, responsive, and supportive school environment. We believe that a great learning experience starts with open communication between the school administration, students, parents, and staff.

Our Services

Your voice matters. Choose the complaint category that fits your concern, and our team will handle it with care, fairness, and confidentiality.

Academic Complaint

Complaints regarding scheduling conflicts between the dual curriculum (vocational high school studies and *diniyah* religious text studies), as well as limited time to focus on vocational skills and industrial work practice (*prakerin*).

Facility Complaint

Complaints regarding insufficient supporting facilities, whether for daily living needs in the boarding school or technical equipment for vocational practice in the school.

Anonymous Complaint

Complaints that are highly sensitive or involve serious violations, where the reporter conceals their identity for their own safety from pressure by administrators or seniors.

FAQ

Find quick answers to the most common questions about submitting a report, how it is processed, and how we keep your information safe.

Select the category that matches the damaged facility, such as classrooms, dormitories, or workshop equipment, so your report reaches the right team.

Describe the building, room, or area as specifically as possible. Attaching a photo helps officers find and assess the problem faster.

You will be notified every time the status of your report changes, from received and in progress to resolved.

Your report is only accessible to authorized officers, and the anonymous option lets you keep your identity hidden.

Each report is assigned to the officer responsible for its category, and you can track its progress on the report status page.

After you submit it, your report is verified, forwarded to the relevant officer, followed up on, and marked as resolved once completed.
