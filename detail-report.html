Schedule Conflict Report

By: Overcommited | 2 months ago

The students struggle to manage their time because their extracurricular commitments coincide with the deadlines for projects involving machine assembly or web application development.

We are not asking for fewer responsibilities, only a schedule that lets us fulfill them properly.

As a result, many students must work late into the night to finish their projects, leaving them exhausted and less focused during lessons. Some projects are submitted incomplete, while others suffer in quality because there is simply not enough time to refine them. We hope the school can review the activity calendar and coordinate project deadlines with extracurricular schedules, so students have enough time to prepare and give their best in every commitment.
