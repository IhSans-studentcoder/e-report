How to Use IDN E-Report

To make a report, simply go to the All Reports page (the "Let's Start!" button on the homepage) and click the "Add Report" button. After doing so, you will be asked to fill the forum. In it, you need to write the title, pick the category that fits your report, add a clear description, and then upload an image of the issue you want to report

Step-By-Step Guide

01
Open the All Reports page

Go to the All Reports page from the homepage and click the Add Report button to begin your submission.

02
Click the "Add Complaint" Button

Click the Add Report button to start the process of submitting your complaint or report.

03
Provide Complaint Details

Now in here, you would need to add the details of your complaint as detailed as possible, so we can know what happened as detailed as possible. So you must Write the title, pick the right category, and provide a detailed description of the issue. And after that you must upload an image of the situation or the report.

04
Submit and Share

After you're finished writing the forum, review your forum one last time, submit it, and let your report appear on the report list for others to see.
